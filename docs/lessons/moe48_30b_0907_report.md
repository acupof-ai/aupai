## 5. The 26-29k excursion and its falsified hypotheses

The aggregate val rose a second time: 1.846 at 23600 to a peak of 1.884 at 29600, then ten consecutive descents to 1.845 at 31600. b0's fit of the reversal: −0.01015/1k at t=−10.92 over 29800-31600 at full stable lr, refit without the extreme point.

The three hypotheses b0 falsified for this excursion exist only as peer messages to 4c on 2026-09-08 — no ledger row, no prereg amendment, no artifact. b0 confirmed this by check (message to 98, 2026-09-08) and asked that they be reported as unpersisted. They are:

1. **Composition** (the 160-row draw shifted toward worse domains): bounded at ≤0.295pp of the rise.
2. **Epoch wrap** (a domain's cursor wrapped its pool and began re-reading): none occurred.
3. **Repetition** (re-reading rows degrades them): the per-domain repetition correlation was r=+0.43, but zh_web — at 0.033 epochs, effectively unrepeated — rose second-fastest, which kills the repetition account.

Two of these are negative results, the class that vanishes when it is not written down, and they were not written down. The excursion closed on its own before the stop, with no intervention applied (b0, same message). The first excursion's decomposition (section 4) is the only one with a ledger row; the second has none, and no per-domain read was run for it.

## 8. Throughput and MFU

Steady state from the pod log's clean intervals: 46K tok/s/gpu, 2.83 s/step, peak 44.1 GiB/card. The leg delivered 25,000 steps in 20.6 training-hours (11.1h for 13,500 steps to the stop, 9.5h for 11,500 after it), ~2.97 s/step including save/val dips.

The printed MFU (~278%) is not an efficiency and must not be quoted as one. `train.py:3792` computes `6 * n_dense * tps / peak`, and `n_dense` (train.py:3258) subtracts only memory-table params — all 48 routed experts count as if every token traversed them, so the number is arithmetically above 100% of the 148 TFLOPS bf16 peak. On active params (~0.2B) the same throughput is about 37% of peak. The fix is PR #40 (de-71, open; task de-71 in runs/tasks.jsonl): price active FLOPs, leave the dense arm's denominator alone.

The planning ratio stands: MoE-48 costs 1.263-1.283x the dense arm's step time at matched batch (facts/efficiency.json#eff.moe48_dense_step_cost_ratio), and the whole gap is overhead and shape, never extra arithmetic — active FLOPs are equal by construction ((top_k+shared)×expert_ffn = 4×768 = 3072 = ffn_hidden, facts/efficiency.json#eff.moe48_step_cost_breakdown).

## 10. Verdict and what changes in v2

好：

- Val ended at 1.824, −0.073 from the leg's first reading (1.897 at 9200), and the descent re-established itself after both excursions. The stop trigger never fired.
- The epoch-ceiling defect was found cardless and fixed before resume; the cap's measured cost saturated (cot slope 0.13x by window 2) and the code domains the objective names stayed flat (starcoder −0.0002/1k in window 1).
- Routing stayed healthy for the whole leg (section 9), and the run held 44.1 GiB/card with no OOM or NaN.

坏：

- No annealed endpoint. The stop landed 332 steps pre-warmdown, so every endpoint number is constant-LR while the 8B bar's lambada side is annealed; the endpoint comparison is not like-for-like.
- The stop/restart and the mix change are confounded: 38% of the first excursion rose on domains whose weight did not change, and no control separates restart from mix.
- The second excursion's three falsified hypotheses and the reversal statistic exist only in peer messages (section 5). Negative results that vanish twice — once from the record, once from the next person's priors.
- The resume source has no domain_bpb (mid-run co-residency refusal), so the endpoint's domain_bpb has no same-run starting point.
- ~2 of 22.8 elapsed hours went to launch failures, the core dump, and drift-gate refusals (section 9).

v2:

1. Per-run epoch ceiling in the mix generator before launch — this run found it at step 9,900.
2. Do not cross a stop boundary and a mix change at the same step; the 38% unseparated is the price.
3. Merge PR #40 before quoting any MoE efficiency number.
4. Score the resume source's full panel before the run starts, on a free card.
5. Register excursion hypotheses in the prereg row, not in messages.
6. Complete the warmdown, or register a constant-LR bar before the endpoint reading exists.
