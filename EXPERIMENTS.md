## What each run taught us

### doc_mask_ab — no_doc_mask 9m04s / docmask 8m18s, both exit 0, both 32 recompiles
- **Asked:** Does --doc_mask (default ON since d5db37b) actually work on GPU, and what does the varlen FlashKDA path cost? It had never been run on a GPU, and the concern was a torch.compile recompile storm from the per-batch cu_seqlens length plus the FlashKDA 'A_log must be float32' error on the varlen path.
- **Learned:** doc_mask is free. mark_dynamic(cu, 0) does its job: the two arms recompile exactly the same number of times, so the varying cu_seqlens length triggers nothing. The 32 recompiles come from Block.forward specializing on mixer type (guard: ___check_type_id(self._modules['mixer']) type=DeltaRecurrence), which is 2 variants over 12 layers and is unrelated to doc_mask. No FlashKDA A_log error — the .float() patch is still in the pod's site-packages, which is itself the risk: it is not in the repo and dies with the container.
- **So:** Keep doc_mask ON for the pretrain. Neither arm's wall-clock is a throughput measurement — both were dominated by compile and by the epoch-end validation, which is what led to capping that validation at Cfg.val_batches_full.

### ab2_attnres_vs_base — both arms died at 2m43s: CUDA OOM, 94.9/95.2 GiB on every rank
- **Asked:** Does Attention Residuals (softmax over depth instead of the residual sum) actually lower loss at 200M/L=12, and what does it cost in throughput? The earlier base-only arm ran on stale code (no doc_mask, old dt_bias init, short_conv in the wrong LR group), so both arms are re-run on current code to make the comparison mean anything.
- **Learned:** The A/B never ran; what it found instead is that Cfg.batch=72 with Cfg.grad_ckpt=False does not fit on an H20 at all. Batch 48 OOMs identically (93.5 GiB), so the earlier 6-GPU ablation_base that reached 500 steps at batch 72 must have run the pod's older train.py, back when grad_ckpt still defaulted to True — the default was flipped to False without re-measuring what batch that leaves room for.
- **So:** Ablations move to --grad_ckpt. An adversarial review then measured that Full AttnRes at L=12 makes Inductor save one [B,T,D] tensor per (consumer, source) pair — 325 of them, 196 GB/GPU at batch 72 — so the sweep now compares base / attn_res_blocks=4 / attn_res_blocks=2 / Full rather than assuming Full is affordable.

### rlvr_gspo — stopped at step 90/500: acc 1.6-2.3%, gnorm 0.35-0.43, ~30% of groups degenerate
- **Learned:** A second copy of the run survived the first stop (duplicate launch on port 29541) and got to step 90 — 90 steps of flat accuracy with a stable gradient norm confirms the anti-collapse fixes hold, and equally confirms there is no signal to amplify: 1.6-2.3% sampled at T=0.9 against 31% greedy on the same distribution from the same checkpoint. Gold quality is not the cause (22-agent audit: 0.84% bad).
- **So:** Abandon RL on the old architecture. Pivot to a fresh pretrain on the new architecture (AttnRes + fused GEMMs + doc_mask + domain mix), because a 31%-greedy/2%-sampled base has no exploitable pass@k gap regardless of the RL objective.

### sft_v5_hard — math-hard: 32/1032 = 3.1% (L3 4%, L4 3%)
- **Learned:** The hard eval separates checkpoints that math-500 cannot: v4 scored 0.8% and v5 3.1% there, while on math-500 they are 32.0% vs 31.0% (indistinguishable). Absolute level is still near-zero — the model gets almost every arithmetic step wrong on L3/L4.
- **So:** math_hard_eval_1k is now the primary metric; math-500 is kept only as a regression tripwire.

### rlvr_stage2 — math-500 11.2% COLLAPSED, ckpt deleted
- **Asked:** Can GRPO with a verifiable boxed reward lift a 32% model?
- **Learned:** No — collapsed to 11.2%. Three causes, none of them the choice of RL algorithm: T=0.1 made the 8 samples per prompt near-identical so most groups had std=0 and zero advantage; there was no KL term or reference model, so 500 steps drifted unanchored; and at 32% pass@1 there was little gap between pass@1 and pass@8 for RL to amplify.
- **So:** No RL until SFT clears ~60% and pass@8 minus pass@1 is at least 15 points. When RL resumes: sequence-level ratio (GSPO), T around 0.8, KL beta 0.01-0.04, and only train on prompts with a 20-80% solve rate (the LFM-1.3B-Math selection rule).

### sft_v2_stage1 — math-500 26.4% (worse: lost openo1/school_math_r1 volume)
- **Asked:** Redo stage-1 from the pretrain checkpoint using only verified short-format data — better than the original mix?
- **Learned:** No — 26.4%. The rebuilt mix dropped openo1 (77K) and the full-length school_math_r1_zh (223K) in exchange for 110K filtered rows. Filtering raised per-row quality and lowered total information; accuracy followed the total, not the quality.
- **So:** Never let a quality filter shrink the corpus without replacing the volume. Verify aggressively, then backfill to at least the original token count.

### sft_stage1 — math-500 32.2% (baseline)
- **Asked:** What does the model score on Chinese grade-school math after a normal instruction SFT?
- **Learned:** 32.2% on the 500-problem holdout. The CoT is fluent and well-formed; the arithmetic inside it is wrong. Fluency and correctness are decoupled at this scale.
- **So:** Treat 32.2% as the number every later experiment must beat. Stop reading loss as progress — only holdout accuracy counts.

### sft_math_stage2 — math-500 32.2%

### sft_short_stage2 — math-500 31.6% (format alone no gain)

- **So:** Keep the short format for cheaper generation and cleaner verification, but stop expecting accuracy from formatting work.

### sft_v4 — TOTAL math-500: 160/500 = 32.0%
- **Asked:** With Ape210K capped at 10% and openo1/s1k restored, does 2.4x the loss tokens of v3 beat the 32.2% baseline?
- **Learned:** Recovered the regression (32.0%) but did not beat the baseline, despite 175M loss tokens against v3's 72M. Three runs now sit within 0.2 points of 32%: this is a plateau, not noise. Generation is clean (132 chars, 89.6% boxed) and still arithmetically wrong. The corpus is also less diverse than its row count suggests — deduplication dropped 126K of Mxode's 220K rows as Belle prompts rewritten.
- **So:** Stop adding data drawn from the same distribution. Next: (a) the L3/L4 hard eval set, to establish whether 32% is a model ceiling or holdout saturation, since all 500 holdout problems come from Belle; (b) genuinely heterogeneous data — TinyGSM's 11.8M rows are MIT-licensed and share no distribution with Belle; (c) drop grad_ckpt now that the NaN cause is fixed, for ~25% faster iteration.

### sft_v3 — TOTAL math-500: 134/500 = 26.8%
- **Asked:** Does 489K verified rows from open Chinese datasets (Ape210K, Belle, Mxode, math23k) beat the baseline?
- **Learned:** No — 26.8%, a regression. Ape210K was 32% of the mix and its rows are a single line ('列式：5/3 = 1.667') with no intermediate steps. Average generation fell to 136 characters and the model started answering division with quotient-and-remainder where the gold answer is a fraction. A large share of one-line data actively destroys multi-step reasoning.
- **So:** Cap any single-line source at roughly 10% of the mix. Judge a math corpus by the step count of its solutions, not by its row count.

### perf_sweep — batch 32=29s/10步, 64=57s/10步 -> 吞吐恒定271K tok/s (compute-bound); no_grad_ckpt NaN -> 根因见 850369a
- **Asked:** Where is SFT throughput going, and why does disabling activation checkpointing produce NaN?
- **Learned:** Throughput is compute-bound at ~271K tok/s across 6 H20s — doubling the batch doubles step time exactly, so batch tuning is a dead end. The NaN has nothing to do with FP8 range (input amax 936 against an e4m3 limit of 448, scaling correct): Inductor's min-cut partitioner recomputes FP8LinearFunction's saved fp8 tensors in the backward graph, re-dividing already-scaled values. Isolated by bisection — eager is clean at batch 2 and 8 with Muon and 130 steps; adding torch.compile NaNs at step 1 with a finite forward and all 152 parameter gradients NaN.
- **So:** @torch._dynamo.disable on FP8Linear.forward (commit 850369a); verified 60 clean steps with compile on and grad_ckpt off. Activation checkpointing can now be dropped for roughly 25% more speed. Stop tuning batch size for throughput.
