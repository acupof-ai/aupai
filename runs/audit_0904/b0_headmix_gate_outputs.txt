WHAT THE PASS BOUNDS. Two limits, both measured on this hardware, both of which the fixture
got wrong first. Stating them because "bitwise identical" would overclaim the result.

1. ONE BF16 ULP IN A WEIGHT IS THE FLOOR. Weights are stored fp32 and the forward runs under
   bf16 autocast. One fp32 ulp at 0.017333984375 is 0.01733398623764515; bf16's neighbouring
   value is 0.0174560546875, ~700x further. The first fixture stepped one fp32 ulp and BOTH
   sites reported IDENTICAL -- autocast rounded the perturbation away before the kernel saw it.
   A change below one bf16 step is invisible here by construction.

2. AND THE UNIT IS A ROW, NOT AN ELEMENT. After fixing (1), the KDA site STILL reported
   IDENTICAL: one bf16 ulp on blocks.0.mixer.qkv.weight[0] alone moved the logits by exactly
   0.0. Not a saturation artifact -- at the argmax the logit is 8.905232 and NO logit is within
   0.01 of SOFTCAP=15 (model.py:63, :554). It is the path's real sensitivity: that element
   supplies 1 of d=768 terms in a dot product, one bf16 ulp there is dw=1.2207e-04, and the
   pre-activation it lands in is stored at bf16 whose ulp near 1.0 is ~4e-3 -- roughly 30x
   larger, so the store rounds it away.

   Measured on the same weight, same seed, same card, to rule out a dead path:
     one element, one bf16 ulp      max|diff| 0.000000e+00
     one element x1.5               max|diff| 1.634884e-01   (3,184,470 of 4,194,944 nonzero)
     one element x2.0               max|diff| 1.634884e-01   (3,198,005 of 4,194,944 nonzero)
     whole row (128 el, 1 ulp each) max|diff| 1.485295e-01
   The element is on a live path. x1.5 and x2.0 share a max but differ in nonzero count, so
   they are different outputs whose maxima coincide, not a clamp.

   MLA's kv_down fired on a single element only because its rows are narrower. Accepting that
   would have shipped a gate whose KDA half never discriminated -- and KDA is the half arm B
   changes. So both sites step row 0.

Lesson, one line: A FIXTURE MUST PERTURB AT THE RESOLUTION AND THE GRANULARITY OF THE PATH IT
CERTIFIES. Resolution was the first defect (fp32 vs bf16), granularity the second (element vs
row). Both produced a green gate on a model that had genuinely changed.
